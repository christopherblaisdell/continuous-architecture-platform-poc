## Performance Notes

### PC (Desktop)

- Games that target 30 FPS on PS4 will often hit 30 FPS on mid-range PCs without issue
- 60 FPS PS4 titles may require a modern GPU for full speed
- CPU single-thread performance matters more than core count
- Vulkan driver quality matters -- latest drivers recommended

### Steam Deck

- The Steam Deck's AMD APU handles many indie and 2D games at full speed
- AAA titles generally run at 20-30 FPS, which is playable for slower-paced games
- Battery life varies significantly -- expect 1-3 hours depending on the game
- Setting TDP limits and frame rate caps via Steam's performance overlay can help stability
- See [Steam Deck Setup](../guides/steam-deck-setup.md) and [Performance Optimization](../guides/performance-optimization.md) for tuning tips
