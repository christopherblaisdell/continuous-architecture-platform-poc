# Notable In-Game Titles

## Games That Run But Aren't Fully Playable Yet

These titles are rated **Ingame** on shadPS4 -- they get past menus and into actual gameplay, but have issues that prevent a complete or smooth experience. Many of these are AAA titles that represent the next wave of games likely to reach Playable status.

!!! info "Why This List Matters"
    Ingame titles are the most likely to become Playable with future shadPS4 updates. If your favorite game is here, keep an eye on new releases.

## What Keeps These Games from "Playable"?

The most common issues preventing Ingame titles from reaching Playable status:

### Graphical Issues
- **Missing effects** -- Shadows, particles, reflections, or post-processing effects may not render
- **Texture corruption** -- Some textures may display incorrectly or as garbage data
- **Shader compilation stutters** -- First-time shader compilation can cause severe framerate dips

### Performance Issues
- **Low framerate** -- Many AAA games run at 10-20 FPS even on powerful hardware
- **Memory usage** -- Some games exceed available VRAM or system RAM
- **CPU bottlenecks** -- Complex game logic can overwhelm single-thread performance

### Stability Issues
- **Random crashes** -- Games may crash after varying amounts of playtime
- **Progression blockers** -- Specific cutscenes, boss fights, or areas may crash consistently
- **Save corruption** -- Some games may fail to save or load properly

### Missing Functionality
- **Audio problems** -- Music or sound effects may be missing, distorted, or out of sync
- **Input issues** -- Certain control schemes or input methods may not work correctly
- **Network stubs** -- Games requiring online checks may fail even for single-player content

## Tracking Progress

The boundary between Ingame and Playable shifts with every shadPS4 update. To track progress:

1. **Watch the shadPS4 blog** -- [shadps4.net/blog](https://shadps4.net/blog) covers newly working titles
2. **Check the compatibility database** -- [shadps4.net/compatibility](https://shadps4.net/compatibility) is updated by community testers
3. **Follow the Discord** -- Real-time reports of game compatibility changes
4. **Review release notes** -- Each shadPS4 version notes which games are fixed
